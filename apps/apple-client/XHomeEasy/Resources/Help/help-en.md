# X Home Easy Help

X Home Easy helps owners, architects, and interior designers in the United States and Canada decide on a wired estate system. It does not operate the lights, and it does not replace a licensed electrician or an authorized integrator.

Ranking uses fit and evidence, not commission. Budgets are ranges. After an OvrC transfer, a Control4 household can change Smart Home Pro at customer.control4.com. This help does not describe every brand as the same kind of lock-in.

Verified 2026-10-21. Any price below is a planning assumption from the requirements, not a live checkout price.

## Workflow

1. Sign in with Apple. Sign out ends the session and keeps the plan. Delete account removes that account’s records on this iPhone.
2. Answer the six questions, or use “Describe my house from an example” and edit a reference into your own house.
3. Read the three paths: professional wired, retail Matter / Apple Home, and hybrid. Each has words, not color alone.
4. On Plan, set rooms, choose Good / Better / Best, and read the comparison and the six-bucket band.
5. On RFQ, enter city and ZIP only, create the PDF, and line up at most three bids.
6. On Integrators, filter the public sample by state and brand. It is not a national or CEDIA-complete directory.
7. Check the handover items for the brands you selected. Confirmation stays off until every item is checked.
8. On Care, track the schedule, subscription questions, and an inherited system.

A hotel of guest rooms does not use the house workflow, and a house band is not multiplied by the number of keys.

## Advisor

The six questions are size, stage, rack room, budget, needs, and what you already own. Sizes also show an approximate square-meter band. “Budget not stated” produces no dollar figures.

The result shows three paths labeled Lead, Consider, or Not the lead. The score is a heuristic, not a manufacturer certification. If drywall is still open, the app names the pre-wire window and does not explain how to pull cable.

The on-device advisor can discuss changing integrator, brand differences, and published budget bands. Questions about line-voltage wiring, an electrical panel, or bypassing Composer Pro, Configure Pro, or Savant dealer tools are refused. Ask a licensed electrician or an authorized integrator. The advisor is not a legal opinion or a permit.

## Examples

“Describe my house from an example” is a comparison, not a copy of someone else’s project. The examples are a new house of about 600 square meters, a 30-year house of about 300 square meters, an estate over 2,000 square meters, a five-star hotel, and a small house that specifies Basalte keypads.

Size, stage, and needs start from the example and are then yours to change. The room list stays as it is unless you ask to start from the example. Examples can be removed and restored.

## Plan

Plan holds room cards, Good / Better / Best, the lighting base and control host, the six-bucket band, ten price questions, the four-brand comparison, subsystem cards, and fusion cards.

The buckets are structured wiring, lighting and shades, audio and theater, control hardware and programming, and security, network, HVAC, and pool. Figures are ranges. Programming is often described as 15–25% of the project. Labor, programming, and rack finish together are often described as 30–55%. Those bands were reviewed on 2026-10-21 from trade press and service firms. They are not a manufacturer price. A licensed integrator’s site quote controls.

If no budget is stated, no dollars are shown. Specified Basalte keypads are not silently rewritten as Lutron. European and Middle East cards wear a region label. E4 community notes stay folded and are not specifications.

## RFQ, bids, and handover

The RFQ asks for city, state or province, and ZIP. The PDF includes the disclaimer and the safety boundary.

Up to three bids line up on hardware, pre-wire, labor, programming hours and rate, rack and network, whether a subscription is mandatory, and whether handover is accepted. A lower total with programming hours well under the reference band is marked as a change-order risk. Rows marked Sample are not a company’s bid.

Handover is by brand:

- Control4: OvrC ownership, customer.control4.com, Linked Resources rebuilt by the next Pro, and whether Connect and Assist are optional.
- Crestron: Crestron Home or SIMPL. If SIMPL is included, whether the uncompiled source transfers at final payment.
- Savant: Host backup, and how Essentials is released when the dealer changes. A public Essentials price was not verified.
- Lutron: the HomeWorks project file, admin login, and engraving schedule. That list is not applied when Lutron is not selected.

Confirmation stays on this iPhone. It is not an e-signature and not a legal opinion.

## Integrators

Filter by name, city, state or province, and brand. Sort is evidence, then name, not commission. A sponsored placement must say Sponsored and is not moved to the top. This offline directory has none.

The page states that the list is a public sample as of 2026-10-21 plus later claims. It is not a national or CEDIA-complete directory, and row counts are not market share. A license note is only as fresh as the fetch date. Check with the state before you sign. A claim interest stays on this iPhone for now.

## Care

Six stages: design freeze, pre-wire window, locations before paint, equipment and rack, programming, and training with handover. The list is a reminder. It does not monitor a job site and does not give wiring steps.

The subscription radar is a checklist for Control4 Connect, Assist, Savant Essentials, and a local service plan: in use or not, who pays, and whether renewal is optional. This app does not bill a manufacturer.

## Account

Sign in with Apple. The name and email arrive only on the first authorization.

Sign out ends the session. That account’s plan remains on this iPhone.

Delete account removes the on-device sign-in record and that account’s plan, consult, quote drafts, and settings on this iPhone. It does not delete the Apple ID, and this iPhone cannot restore the deleted plan.

## Free and paid

Owners are not asked to pay a monthly fee in order to finish the fit result. The paid owner right is a one-time package, not a monthly membership.

The amounts below are assumptions in the 2026-10-21 requirements and still need testing. They are not prices this app collects. This build does not take payment. Until payment is turned on, the functions remain available on the device. After payment is turned on, the split is below.

### Owners

Free includes:

- The fit summary and the three paths
- The basic four-brand comparison, evidence badges, and source links
- The public integrator directory
- The safety boundary and the on-device advisor
- Example-guided description
- The consult draft stored on this iPhone

The one-time package includes (assumed $79–$199 per package, not a list price, and not yet charged):

- A deep plan report PDF
- The full RFQ package
- A workbook comparing up to three bids

Free does not include those three exports. Neither tier includes a lowest-price promise, installation labor, collecting a manufacturer subscription, or moving a sponsor to the top of search.

### Integrators

Public directory fields stay visible to owners without a fee.

Integrator Pro is a separate account, assumed at $99–$199 per month and still to be tested. The intended rights are a desk, incoming RFQs, structured bids, and a lead dashboard. A founder mark does not change sort order. This build has no cloud desk and does not charge integrators.

## Evidence

- E1 Official: a public page from the brand or the standards body.
- E2 Industry: an association, a certification, or a trade-press review.
- E3 Integrator: a portfolio or a claim, not a manufacturer promise.
- E4 Community: folded by default, and not a specification or a handover policy by itself.
